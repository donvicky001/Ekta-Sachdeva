03Story

About

Healthcare sales and marketing, working together.

I grow healthcare businesses through a blend of sales execution and data-driven marketing. My focus is patient acquisition and clinic growth, building pipelines through corporate health partnerships, on-ground sales enablement, and integrated campaigns across Meta, Google, SEO, CRM, and referral loops.

In clinics and hospitals, I work end-to-end: shaping offers and patient journeys, aligning with medical and operations teams, and setting up clear reporting on leads, bookings, conversion, and retention. I’m hands-on with creative, copy, landing pages, and tracking.

In aesthetics and wellness, I bring a premium experience lens: brand narratives, events, influencer programmes, and high-touch activations that lift perception while staying accountable to bookings and repeat visits.

What I bringcorporate outreach that feeds the funnel, campaigns and CRM that convert, field enablement so teams can close, and simple, transparent reporting so decisions are fast and grounded.

Influencer teaser: the mystery box

Selected influencers received a branded mystery box with no explanation: just a sealed card and some health products, asking “Do you like sweet surprises?” It built intrigue before launch.

The idea

A campaign that shocked, educated, and converted, aimed at Endocrinology, Ophthalmology, and Dietician services.

The activation

A classic vending machine re-engineered as a health awareness tool. Instead of snacks, it revealed the hidden sugar in everyday drinks by dispensing that many sugar cubes.

The result

Placed at the clinic launch, it drove 6,470 footfall through free HbA1c sign-ups, with 6.9M reach, 8.4M views, and 32 press coverages.

06 / 06Aster Dental · Innovation · AI · WhatsApp

SmylAI

A free AI oral screening on WhatsApp, in under two minutes, that turns awareness into appointments.

521%ROI
~19KAED spent
~118KAED revenue
944hot leads
2 minto a 6-indicator AI report

The problem

People delay or skip dental check-ups because of low awareness, cost worries, and no easy self-screening tool. They arrive late, when treatment is costlier and harder to reverse.

The solution

An intelligent WhatsApp bot runs AI oral screenings at scale, checking cavities, bleeding gums, stains, sensitivity, calculus, and bad breath, and a tele-dentistry model that makes screening available to everyone.

Who it works for

  • Patients: a free, instant check from their phone
  • Dentists: an AI report and images before the visit
  • Concierge: a structured follow-up workflow
  • Marketing: defined nudges for new visits, repeat visits, and cross-selling
